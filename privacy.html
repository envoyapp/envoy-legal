Envoy Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Envoy is an end-to-end encrypted messaging app for iOS, Android, and the web. In the app, messages are called notes. This policy explains what data Envoy handles, where it goes, and how to remove it.

What you don't need to give us

Envoy has no sign-up. It does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number, and it does not read your device's address book. You are identified by an anonymous ID made of three words.

What is stored on our servers

  • Your ID and public encryption key, so that contacts can find you and encrypt to you. A random sign-in identifier links your install to your ID.
  • Last-seen time, used to show whether a contact is available.
  • Encrypted notes and photos waiting for delivery. These are encrypted on your device with keys the servers never have, so we cannot read them. They are removed after your contact's device receives them. An item that is never picked up, for example because the recipient stopped using the app, can stay on the server in its encrypted form.
  • A notification token, only if you turn on Notifications in Settings. It lets our server ask Google (and, on iPhone, Apple) to show a notification on your device. Notifications never contain the note, the sender, or your ID: they only say that an update is available. Turning Notifications off, or deleting all data, removes the token.
  • A moved account, briefly. When you move your account to another device, your ID, keys, and contact list are sent through our server, encrypted with the one-time code shown on your old device. The server cannot read them. They are deleted when the move finishes, and can no longer be fetched after 10 minutes in any case.
  • Connection setup messages, used to start a direct connection between two devices. These are short-lived.
  • Reports you send. If you report a contact, we store your ID, their ID, the reason, and anything you type in the details box, so that we can review it. Your notes are not included.

Our servers can see technical details such as which IDs are communicating, when, and how much data is sent. They cannot see the contents.

IP addresses and direct connections

Like any internet service, our providers process your IP address when your device connects. When two contacts are connected directly, each device can see the other's IP address. You can turn this off in Settings under Direct connections, and notes will then go through the server instead.

What stays on your device

Your notes, photos, and contacts are stored on your device. On the web, they are stored in your browser's storage for the Envoy site. Your private key is stored encrypted, protected by your password. The note database itself is not separately encrypted by the app, so it relies on your device's own security.

Camera and photos

Envoy uses your camera or photo library only when you choose to take, attach, or save a photo. Photos are encrypted before they leave your device.

What we don't do

Envoy has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not sell data or share it with data brokers.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Storage, and Cloud Functions) runs the backend.
  • DigitalOcean hosts the relay server used when two devices can't connect directly. Relayed data remains encrypted.

Deleting your data

  • Delete All Data in Settings permanently removes the app's data from your device, and clears your public key from the server. Your ID stays reserved so that nobody else can take it over.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device. On the web, clearing the site's data in your browser does the same.
  • Entering the wrong password three times also wipes all data on the device.
  • Auto-delete in Settings can remove notes from your device on a schedule. This does not affect your contact's copy.

Items waiting on our servers are deleted automatically: encrypted notes and photos after 30 days at most, connection setup messages within minutes.

If you no longer have the app

Email alexander.john@gmail.com with your ID and ask for your data to be deleted. We will remove your public key and notification token from the server and retire your ID, just as Delete All Data does. IDs are anonymous, so we can't confirm that you own one; please only send your own.

There is no password recovery. Because we don't hold your keys, a forgotten password can't be reset.

Blocking

Blocking a contact is stored only on your device. We aren't told, and neither is the person you blocked.

Children

Envoy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email alexander.john@gmail.com or open an issue. Issues are public, so please don't include your ID or anything private.
