Envoy Support

Help with the Envoy app for iOS and Android

Getting started

Choose a password the first time you open Envoy. Share your ID with someone, and add theirs with the New button. In the app, messages are called notes.

I forgot my password

It can't be recovered, because we never have your keys. Your data can't be restored.

Why did I see a network error at the password screen?

This message appears when the password is wrong. After three wrong passwords, Envoy permanently wipes all of its data on that device.

A contact looks offline or my notes are slow

Make sure both of you are on the latest version and have the app open. Notes are delivered when the other person next opens the app, and Envoy doesn't send notifications.

Deleting everything

Open Settings and choose Delete All Data. This can't be undone.

Contact us

Open an issue to report a problem or abusive behavior. Issues are public, so please don't include your ID or anything private.
